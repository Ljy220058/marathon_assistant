\section{Experimental Setup}

\subsection{Systems and Models}

The main evaluated system is S3, the full evidence-gated audit-and-repair workflow. The primary local model is \texttt{qwen2.5:latest}. \texttt{llama3:latest} is used as a supplementary sanity check, not as evidence of broad model generalization.

\subsection{Primary Runs}

The primary 100-question main benchmark uses \texttt{qwen2.5:latest} with \texttt{retrieval\_plus\_gold}; its pre-gate mode is off. The safety stress suite uses \texttt{qwen2.5:latest} and \texttt{llama3:latest} with \texttt{retrieval\_only}; its pre-gate mode is \texttt{hardening\_v0\_4}. A supplementary 40-question llama3 main-benchmark subset uses the same balanced qids and \texttt{retrieval\_plus\_gold}.

\subsection{v0.3 Ablation Subset}

We also ran a balanced 40-question v0.3 subset ablation with \texttt{qwen2.5:latest}. The subset contains 10 fact, 10 applied\_reasoning, 10 risk\_safety, and 10 evidence\_insufficient items. It is the same balanced qid set used for the llama3 sanity check. Three ablations are reported: \texttt{no\_gate}, \texttt{no\_audit}, and \texttt{no\_repair}.

\subsection{Metrics}

Primary output statuses are answered, partial\_answer, and refused. Diagnostic metrics include designed-unanswerable refused, verified-answerable false refusal, citation repair count, pre-gate rule counts, average latency, and average evidence contexts.
