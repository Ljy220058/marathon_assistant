## 摘要/引言要点
Abstract Objective To estimate the relative efficacy of individual and combinations of prehabilitation components (exercise, nutrition, cognitive, and psychosocial) on critical outcomes of postoperative complications, length of stay, health related quality of life, and physical recovery for adults who have received surgery. Design Systematic review with network and component network meta-analyses of randomised controlled trials. Data sources Medline, Embase, PsycINFO, CINAHL, Cochrane Library, and Web of Science were initially searched 1 March 2022, and updated on 25 October 2023. Certainty in findings were assessed using the Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis (CINeMA) approach. Main outcom

## 结果
- 核心发现：含运动的多组件 prehab 整体上降低并发症、改善恢复，但证据确定性多为低/极低。

## 局限
- 研究空白与趋势：组件协同、剂量、成本和低偏倚多中心 RCT 不足；趋势：组件化 prehab。
